# Video presentation requirements
1. Format & Technical Specifications 
	• MP4 format, 16:9 aspect ratio preferred, 1080p resolution (minimum 720p). 
	• Audio must be clear, with no background noise. 
	• Duration: 8–10 minutes (strict time limit; overages may result in score deductions).
2. Content Structure 
	• Title & Abstract: Introduce the topic and research question. 
	• Problem Statement: Clearly define the problem and its position/significance with key literatures. 
	• Methodology/Approach: Explain technical details, algorithms, or experimental design. 
	• Results & Analysis: Present findings with visualizations (charts, graphs, tables) and critical interpretation. 
	• Conclusions & Future Work: Summarize contributions and outline next steps. 
3. Delivery Expectations 
	• Speaker should appear on camera (optional but recommended for engagement). 
	• Maintain a professional tone; avoid jargon without explanation. 
	• Use verbal cues (e.g., “Now let’s look at the results”) to guide viewers.
# Slide Deck Requirements
1. Design & Formatting 
	• Slides must be in PDF format, with consistent styling (fonts, colors, layout). 
	• Limit text to bullet points (max 5–7 per slide); prioritize visuals (figures, diagrams) over walls of text. 
	• Include citations for borrowed images or ideas. 
2. Content Prioritization: 
	• Highlight key messages on each slide (e.g., “Key Insight: X outperforms Y by 20%”). 
	• Technical details should be concise but accurate (e.g., equations should be readable and explained). 
	• Avoid overloading slides; aim for 15 slides maximum (excluding references).
