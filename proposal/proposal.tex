\begin{abstract}
We test whether pre-trained facial expression representations from a single \emph{pre-kick} face frame predict penalty-shootout outcomes (goal vs.\ miss), and whether they add value \emph{beyond} historical penalty performance and match context. A 14-match pilot (123 kicks, 114 valid pre-kick faces) is complete; we will train FER-only, context-only, and fused models under leakage-controlled, match-disjoint evaluation of seven falsifiable hypotheses; negative results are valid findings.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and research goal}
Penalty shootouts concentrate high-stakes decisions into isolated kicks, each often preceded by a broadcast close-up of the kicker. Pre-trained facial expression recognition (FER) encoders~\citep{ekman,emotieff} map such faces to emotion probabilities and embeddings, but whether they transfer to low-resolution, domain-shifted sports footage---and predict anything beyond context such as score state and shooting order---is unknown. We compare context-only, FER-only, and fused models under a strict anti-leakage protocol: pre-kick frames only, temporally truncated history, pre-kick scores, and match-disjoint evaluation.

\section{Hypotheses}
(H1) FER-only beats the majority-class baseline; (H2) FER embeddings beat 8-dimensional emotion probabilities; (H3) FER-pretrained beats ImageNet-pretrained encoders under matched architectures; (H4) partial fine-tuning of the last encoder block beats a frozen encoder; (H5) context features (history, round, kick order, pre-kick scores, venue) beat the majority baseline; (H6) FER+Context stably beats context-only---the core test; (H7) random event splits overestimate performance relative to match-disjoint splits.

\section{Data and methods}
Each sample is one shootout kick: a qualified pre-kick face frame (never after ball contact), the outcome label, and context features; historical performance sums a kicker's league penalties over the three \emph{completed} club seasons strictly preceding the match. A 14-match World Cup pilot is complete from broadcast video: 123 kicks, 114 valid faces (93\% retention; 77 goals / 37 misses), every exclusion logged, context verified by a replay validator, and the end-to-end pipeline (timestamping, anti-leakage face capture, FER feature caching) operational. We will extend to 60--80 matches if pre-declared feasibility criteria hold. Models: majority, logistic-regression, and MLP context baselines; emotion-probability and ImageNet-embedding classifiers~\citep{effnet}; frozen and fine-tuned FER classifiers; late fusion.

\section{Evaluation and expected outcomes}
All models share identical match-disjoint 5-fold grouped cross-validation; scalers and encodings are fit on training folds only, and model selection uses validation early stopping only. We report AUROC, AUPRC, balanced accuracy, and Brier score over folds and seeds, plus feature-group, pretraining-source, split-type, and face-quality ablations. Expected contributions: a reusable, leakage-controlled pre-kick face and context pipeline; a quantified feasibility study (retention, cost, class balance); and a controlled answer to whether FER representations add predictive value beyond context---positive or informative negative. Broadcast video and face images will not be redistributed; no mental-state or causal claims will be made.
